Annotate Photos

Outline what you see on clinical photographs. A clinician reviews every annotation before it is released, with credit to you.

Annotation Opens With the Community Platform

Photo queues need sign-in and clinician review, so they run on the community platform. Until it opens, take the training below.
Annotator TrainingRead the guide, learn the terms and pass a short practice task before your first photo.

How It Works

  1. Claim a PhotoIt is yours for 30 minutes and saves as you work.
  2. Mark What You SeeOutline each finding and choose the terms that describe it.
  3. Get ReviewedA clinician approves, edits or returns your work.
  4. Get CreditedApproved work ships in the next release under your name.
