Annotator Training

Four steps from the guide to your first photo. Practice tasks have a known answer, so you see exactly what to improve.

  1. Read the Annotation Guide

    How to outline findings, choose terms and record uncertainty. About ten minutes.

    Read the Guide

  2. Learn the Terms

    Go through the morphology terms and take the quick check until you are confident.

    Learn the Terms

  3. Pass the Practice Tasks

    One short task per queue type, with feedback on every answer. Practice opens with the annotation platform.

  4. Claim Your First Photo

    Each claim is yours for 30 minutes. A clinician reviews your work and you are credited in the next release.
