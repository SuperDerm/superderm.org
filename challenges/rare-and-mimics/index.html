Rare Skin Disease and Common Mimics

Given clinical photographs taken with a phone or camera, can a model include a rare, high-consequence condition in its top three when it resembles a common presentation?

In PreparationNo Submission Deadline Set

Contributions Are Ongoing

Prediction submission opens once the input package, terms of use, reference answers, a pinned scorer and a rerun baseline are ready. Until then you can help by contributing cases and annotations.

Diagnoses in Scope

The clinical team will select the first set of rare, high-consequence diagnoses and the common conditions they resemble.

Inputs and Outputs

Inputs
One or more clinical photographs per case, with an optional short history. Explicit references to the documented diagnosis are removed; clinical findings are kept.
Outputs
Up to three free-text diagnoses in descending order of likelihood, or an explicit abstention (an empty list).

Submission Format (UTF-8 JSONL, One Row per Case)

{"case_id":"s_demo_001","predictions":["Example A","Example B"]}
{"case_id":"s_demo_002","predictions":[]}

An empty list is an explicit abstention. Files with unknown, repeated or missing case IDs, more than three predictions, duplicates or blank entries are rejected with row-level errors and are not counted.

Scoring

Primary Metric
Macro recall@3 over the prespecified rare, high-consequence diagnoses
Secondary
Top-1 and top-3 accuracy over all cases; Top-1 and top-3 accuracy over common mimics
Scorer
Version 1.0.0 · Vocabulary superderm-diagnoses 1.0.0
  • Names are matched after Unicode normalization, case folding and whitespace trimming, against canonical names and approved English synonyms of the versioned vocabulary.
  • Ambiguous or unmapped names count as non-matches. A case scores at most one hit at each k.
  • Accuracy uses the full, fixed number of test cases as the denominator; abstentions score zero and stay in the denominator.
  • Ties on the primary metric receive equal ranks.
