About the Project

SuperDerm brings clinicians and researchers together around clinical photographs of skin disease: phone and camera photos, not dermoscopy or histopathology. A case documented by one clinical team can help another, working far from specialist care, think to consider a diagnosis it might otherwise miss.

Common diseases, rare diseases and the conditions that resemble them are all welcome. Data, models, code and evaluation are released openly, in stages.
