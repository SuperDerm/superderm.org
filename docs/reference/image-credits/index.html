Image Credits

SuperDerm shows two kinds of photographs: clinical photographs from a public dataset whose licence permits public display, and synthetic illustrations used for practice and testing. No photograph from the team's own cases appears on the site.

Photographs from the PAD-UFES-20 Public Dataset#

The photograph on the homepage, the example on Annotate photos, the teaching cases and the preview annotation queue use clinical close-up photographs from PAD-UFES-20, a dataset of skin lesions photographed with smartphones at a dermatology service in Brazil.

  • Creators: Pacheco AGC, Lima GR, Salomão AS, et al. PAD-UFES-20: A skin lesion dataset composed of patient data and clinical images collected from smartphones. Data in Brief. 2020;32:106221. doi:10.1016/j.dib.2020.106221
  • Dataset: Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/zr7vgbcyr2.1
  • Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  • Changes: converted to WebP or JPEG with metadata removed; one image resized. Outlines and boxes are drawn by the site on a separate layer; the photographs themselves are not edited.
  • Diagnoses and histories: the teaching cases show the values recorded in the dataset. Where the dataset marks a lesion as biopsied, the diagnosis is the biopsy result; otherwise it is the clinical diagnosis of the dermatologists who collected the data.

Synthetic Photographs#

The practice tasks and the automated tests use synthetic photographs.

  • They are generated by a script from a fixed seed: procedural images of a skin-toned background with schematic lesion shapes.
  • They depict no patient and no real disease, and each is stamped "SYNTHETIC · NOT A PATIENT".
  • They are released under CC0 1.0. In the platform they are recorded as the source "Synthetic preview photographs".

You may reuse the synthetic photographs freely. They illustrate the workflows; do not present them as clinical images.
