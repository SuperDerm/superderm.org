1f:T17b0,<h1>How Review Works</h1>
<p>Every annotation is checked by an independent clinical reviewer before it can enter a release. This page explains what reviewers decide and what you see as a contributor.</p>
<h2 id="who-reviews">Who Reviews<a class="heading-anchor" href="#who-reviews" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>Reviewers hold the clinical reviewer role for the project, appointed by the clinical lead. See <a href="/docs/getting-started/roles">Roles and permissions</a>.</li>
<li>A reviewer never sees a task they claimed, annotated or skipped, or a case they contributed. Contributors never review their own work.</li>
<li>Reviewers see each assessment labelled &quot;Assessor 1&quot;, &quot;Assessor 2&quot; and so on, not by name, so they judge the work rather than the person.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="when-a-task-reaches-review">When a Task Reaches Review<a class="heading-anchor" href="#when-a-task-reaches-review" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>A task enters the <a href="/contribute">review queue</a> when every independent assessment it needs has been submitted. The queue card on <a href="/annotate">Annotate photos</a> shows how many independent submissions each item needs. Each assessment is decided on its own.</p>
<p>The reviewer sees the photographs with the assessment drawn on them, the terms, &quot;Term not listed&quot; descriptions, <strong>Certain</strong> or <strong>Uncertain</strong>, and any earlier decisions on the task.</p>
<h2 id="decisions">Decisions<a class="heading-anchor" href="#decisions" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Decision</th>
<th>Meaning</th>
<th>Feedback to the contributor</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Approve</td>
<td>The annotation is accurate and follows the guide.</td>
<td>Optional</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Approve with edits</td>
<td>The reviewer corrects a copy: moves shapes or vertices, removes findings, or changes terms and certainty.</td>
<td>Optional</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Return for more information</td>
<td>The contributor revises the annotation and resubmits it.</td>
<td>Required</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Insufficient evidence</td>
<td>The photos do not support a decision.</td>
<td>Required</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>An approved result, with or without edits, can enter the next release if its photographs are cleared for redistribution. Returned and insufficient-evidence assessments never enter a release.</p>
<h2 id="feedback-and-internal-notes">Feedback and Internal Notes<a class="heading-anchor" href="#feedback-and-internal-notes" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Reviewers write two kinds of text:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Feedback to the contributor</strong> is sent to you with the decision and shown in your workspace and in the task.</li>
<li><strong>Internal Note</strong> is for reviewers and leads only. It is never shown to the contributor.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="what-you-see-as-a-contributor">What You See as a Contributor<a class="heading-anchor" href="#what-you-see-as-a-contributor" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>A notification &quot;Review decision for task …&quot; naming the decision, with the reviewer&#39;s feedback.</li>
<li>In <a href="/contribute">My workspace</a>, the review status of each task and the start of the feedback.</li>
<li>If your work was returned, select <strong>Respond to review</strong>. The task opens with <strong>Reviewer feedback</strong> at the top. Edit it and select <strong>Resubmit</strong>. Returned work has no claim timer.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-original-is-always-kept">The Original Is Always Kept<a class="heading-anchor" href="#the-original-is-always-kept" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Approve with Edits</strong> stores the reviewer&#39;s corrected copy as the accepted result, marked &quot;approved with edits&quot;. Your submitted revision is not changed.</li>
<li>A resubmission creates a new revision. The earlier revision is marked superseded, not deleted, and its feedback stays on record.</li>
<li>Every decision is recorded with the reviewer, time, decision, feedback and internal note, and appears in the decision history that reviewers see.</li>
<li>Each revision gets one decision. A second decision on the same revision is refused.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="task-states">Task States<a class="heading-anchor" href="#task-states" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>State</th>
<th>Meaning</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Open</td>
<td>No one has claimed the task yet.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Collecting</td>
<td>At least one slot is claimed; not every independent submission is in.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Ready for review</td>
<td>Every submission is in.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>In review</td>
<td>Some assessments are decided, others are pending.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Accepted</td>
<td>Every assessment was approved, with or without edits.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Returned</td>
<td>At least one assessment was returned to its contributor.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Insufficient evidence</td>
<td>Every assessment is decided, none is returned, and at least one was recorded as insufficient evidence.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Suspended</td>
<td>A photo lost its annotation permission when the batch was reopened.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Each slot of a task is claimed, then submitted. Skipping a task or letting a claim expire releases the slot for someone else.</p>
<p>Your own assessment has its own review status: pending, approved, approved with edits, returned, insufficient evidence, or superseded.</p>
<h2 id="diagnosis-verification">Diagnosis Verification<a class="heading-anchor" href="#diagnosis-verification" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Diagnosis verification is Planned. Two people will make independent assessments of a case, and a credentialed clinical reviewer who took part in neither will adjudicate, using the authorized source evidence. A diagnosis suggested from photographs alone remains a hypothesis, even when assessors agree.</p>
