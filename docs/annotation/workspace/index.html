Using the Workspace

Start Annotating or Resume opens the task in a full-screen workspace: the photo and its toolbar on the left, the panel with instructions and your answers on the right. The panel footer shows the save status and your claim time, with Submit, Save Draft, Skip and Report Privacy Concern.

Tools and Keyboard Shortcuts#

Action Mouse or touch Keyboard
Select, move or reshape Select tool: click a finding, drag it, or drag a white handle V
Draw a box Box tool: drag across the finding B
Draw a polygon Polygon tool: click each vertex, then click the first vertex or double-click to close P. Enter closes, Backspace removes the last vertex, Escape cancels
Undo or redo Toolbar arrows Ctrl/⌘ Z. Redo: Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z or Ctrl/⌘ Y
Delete the selected finding Bin in the toolbar or the findings list Delete or Backspace
Save now Save Draft Ctrl/⌘ S
Switch photo Thumbnail strip under the photo [ and ]
Zoom Mouse wheel, the + and − buttons, Fit + or =, −, and 0 to fit
Pan Drag empty space with Select, hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle button Arrow keys
Nudge a selected vertex or corner Arrow keys: 1 pixel, with Shift 10 pixels

Shortcuts are ignored while you type in a field. Zoom, pan and nudge keys work when the photo has focus: click it or reach it with Tab. Undo covers up to 100 steps of shapes, moves, deletions and term choices, but not typed text.

The panel also offers Draw Box, Draw Polygon, Add Box by Coordinates and Finding Without Location. Image quality tasks have no drawing tools.

Entering Coordinates#

You can place and adjust shapes without a mouse.

  1. Select Add Box by Coordinates. A box appears on the current photo, from 40% to 60% of its width and height.
  2. Open the finding in the list. Under Box coordinates, edit Left, Top, Right and Bottom as percentages of the photo's width and height (0 to 100, steps of 0.1).
  3. For a polygon, Vertices lists x and y for each vertex. Edit a value, remove a vertex (three always remain), or select a vertex number and choose Add Vertex After.
  4. To nudge a vertex with the arrow keys, select its number or handle, then focus the photo.

Coordinates are stored as fractions of the upright photo, from the top-left corner. See Release package format.

Saving#

Your draft saves automatically 2 seconds after your last edit.

Status Meaning
Unsaved changes An edit is waiting to be saved.
Saving… The draft is being sent.
Saved 14:05, or All changes saved The server has your latest edits.
Not saved, with Retry The save failed. Your edits stay in this window; select Retry. If your session ended, sign in again in a new tab first.
Changed in another window See below.

Drafts are kept on the server, not in your browser, so you can resume on any device. Unsaved edits exist only in the open window, and the browser warns you before you leave them.

Conflict between windows. Each save carries a version number. If the task was saved from another window in the meantime, you see "This draft changed in another window. Your edits here are kept until you choose." Select Keep My Edits to save this window's version, or Load the Other Version to discard your edits.

Claim Time#

  • A claim lasts 30 minutes. The footer shows Claim until and the time.
  • Saving renews it: a save with less than 20 minutes left extends the claim to 30 minutes from that moment. Active editing keeps your claim.
  • When the time passes, the slot reopens for others. About 5 minutes later you are notified that the claim expired and your draft is kept.
  • Open the task again and save. If the slot is still free, the platform reclaims it for you. If someone else took it, you see "Your claim on this task expired and the slot was reassigned. Your saved draft is kept and can be viewed, but not submitted." An expired claim never overwrites someone else's claim.

Work returned to you by a reviewer has no claim timer.

Submit#

Select Submit. Your work is checked and any pending edits are saved before it is sent; problems are listed under "Check these before submitting". On success you see "Submitted for independent review" with the task ID and revision. Retrying after a connection failure never creates a second submission.

Skip#

Skip is available while your claim is open. After you confirm, the task returns to the queue for someone else and is never offered to you again. Your draft is not submitted. Skips are recorded separately.

Report Privacy Concern#

Select Report Privacy Concern, describe what may identify a person (a face, tattoo, name or legible text), then select Pause Photo and Report. The photo is paused for everyone at once and a data steward is notified. You cannot submit while a photo is paused: if the steward clears it, it returns; otherwise skip the task. See Report an issue.

Several Photos in One Case#

The toolbar shows "Photo 2 of 3" and the view label. Switch with the thumbnail strip or [ and ]. Each finding belongs to the photo it was drawn on; selecting it in the findings list switches to that photo.

Read-Only and Unavailable#

The workspace turns read-only, with the reason, after you submit or skip, if access was revoked, or while the queue is paused. An unavailable photo shows why, for example "awaiting privacy review".

Phones and Narrow Screens#

Below about 860 pixels wide, the photo sits above the panel and the footer with Submit and Save Draft stays fixed at the bottom of the screen. Draw with your finger, zoom with the + and − buttons, and switch photos with the thumbnail strip.
