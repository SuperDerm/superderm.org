1f:T20a8,<h1>Using the Workspace</h1>
<p><strong>Start Annotating</strong> or <strong>Resume</strong> opens the task in a full-screen workspace: the photo and its toolbar on the left, the panel with instructions and your answers on the right. The panel footer shows the save status and your claim time, with <strong>Submit</strong>, <strong>Save Draft</strong>, <strong>Skip</strong> and <strong>Report Privacy Concern</strong>.</p>

<p>Shortcuts are ignored while you type in a field. Zoom, pan and nudge keys work when the photo has focus: click it or reach it with Tab. Undo covers up to 100 steps of shapes, moves, deletions and term choices, but not typed text.</p>
<p>The panel also offers <strong>Draw Box</strong>, <strong>Draw Polygon</strong>, <strong>Add Box by Coordinates</strong> and <strong>Finding Without Location</strong>. Image quality tasks have no drawing tools.</p>
<h2 id="entering-coordinates">Entering Coordinates<a class="heading-anchor" href="#entering-coordinates" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>You can place and adjust shapes without a mouse.</p>
<ol>
<li>Select <strong>Add Box by Coordinates</strong>. A box appears on the current photo, from 40% to 60% of its width and height.</li>
<li>Open the finding in the list. Under <strong>Box coordinates</strong>, edit <strong>Left</strong>, <strong>Top</strong>, <strong>Right</strong> and <strong>Bottom</strong> as percentages of the photo&#39;s width and height (0 to 100, steps of 0.1).</li>
<li>For a polygon, <strong>Vertices</strong> lists x and y for each vertex. Edit a value, remove a vertex (three always remain), or select a vertex number and choose <strong>Add Vertex After</strong>.</li>
<li>To nudge a vertex with the arrow keys, select its number or handle, then focus the photo.</li>
</ol>
<p>Coordinates are stored as fractions of the upright photo, from the top-left corner. See <a href="/docs/reference/release-format#coordinates">Release package format</a>.</p>
<h2 id="saving">Saving<a class="heading-anchor" href="#saving" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Your draft saves automatically 2 seconds after your last edit.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Status</th>
<th>Meaning</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Unsaved changes</td>
<td>An edit is waiting to be saved.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Saving…</td>
<td>The draft is being sent.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Saved 14:05, or All changes saved</td>
<td>The server has your latest edits.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Not saved, with <strong>Retry</strong></td>
<td>The save failed. Your edits stay in this window; select <strong>Retry</strong>. If your session ended, sign in again in a new tab first.</td>
</tr>
<tr>

</tr>

<p>Drafts are kept on the server, not in your browser, so you can resume on any device. Unsaved edits exist only in the open window, and the browser warns you before you leave them.</p>
<p><strong>Conflict between windows.</strong> Each save carries a version number. If the task was saved from another window in the meantime, you see &quot;This draft changed in another window. Your edits here are kept until you choose.&quot; Select <strong>Keep My Edits</strong> to save this window&#39;s version, or <strong>Load the Other Version</strong> to discard your edits.</p>
<h2 id="claim-time">Claim Time<a class="heading-anchor" href="#claim-time" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>A claim lasts 30 minutes. The footer shows <strong>Claim until</strong> and the time.</li>
<li>Saving renews it: a save with less than 20 minutes left extends the claim to 30 minutes from that moment. Active editing keeps your claim.</li>
<li>When the time passes, the slot reopens for others. About 5 minutes later you are notified that the claim expired and your draft is kept.</li>
<li>Open the task again and save. If the slot is still free, the platform reclaims it for you. If someone else took it, you see &quot;Your claim on this task expired and the slot was reassigned. Your saved draft is kept and can be viewed, but not submitted.&quot; An expired claim never overwrites someone else&#39;s claim.</li>
</ul>
<p>Work returned to you by a reviewer has no claim timer.</p>
<h2 id="submit">Submit<a class="heading-anchor" href="#submit" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Select <strong>Submit</strong>. Your work is checked and any pending edits are saved before it is sent; problems are listed under &quot;Check these before submitting&quot;. On success you see &quot;Submitted for independent review&quot; with the task ID and revision. Retrying after a connection failure never creates a second submission.</p>
<h2 id="skip">Skip<a class="heading-anchor" href="#skip" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p><strong>Skip</strong> is available while your claim is open. After you confirm, the task returns to the queue for someone else and is never offered to you again. Your draft is not submitted. Skips are recorded separately.</p>
<h2 id="report-privacy-concern">Report Privacy Concern<a class="heading-anchor" href="#report-privacy-concern" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Select <strong>Report Privacy Concern</strong>, describe what may identify a person (a face, tattoo, name or legible text), then select <strong>Pause Photo and Report</strong>. The photo is paused for everyone at once and a data steward is notified. You cannot submit while a photo is paused: if the steward clears it, it returns; otherwise skip the task. See <a href="/docs/data/issues">Report an issue</a>.</p>
<h2 id="several-photos-in-one-case">Several Photos in One Case<a class="heading-anchor" href="#several-photos-in-one-case" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>The toolbar shows &quot;Photo 2 of 3&quot; and the view label. Switch with the thumbnail strip or [ and ]. Each finding belongs to the photo it was drawn on; selecting it in the findings list switches to that photo.</p>
<h2 id="read-only-and-unavailable">Read-Only and Unavailable<a class="heading-anchor" href="#read-only-and-unavailable" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>The workspace turns read-only, with the reason, after you submit or skip, if access was revoked, or while the queue is paused. An unavailable photo shows why, for example &quot;awaiting privacy review&quot;.</p>
<h2 id="phones-and-narrow-screens">Phones and Narrow Screens<a class="heading-anchor" href="#phones-and-narrow-screens" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Below about 860 pixels wide, the photo sits above the panel and the footer with <strong>Submit</strong> and <strong>Save Draft</strong> stays fixed at the bottom of the screen. Draw with your finger, zoom with the + and − buttons, and switch photos with the thumbnail strip.</p>
