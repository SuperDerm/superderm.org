1f:T1e7e,<h1>Annotation Guide</h1>
<p>This guide explains what to mark and how to record it. For the controls, see <a href="/docs/annotation/workspace">Using the workspace</a>. Each queue also has its own instructions and guide version, shown on the queue card and in the workspace.</p>
<h2 id="before-you-start">Before You Start<a class="heading-anchor" href="#before-you-start" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>Describe only what the photographs show. Do not share your answers, or look up other people&#39;s answers for the same item.</li>
<li>SuperDerm uses clinical photographs from a phone or camera. If a photo is not a clinical photograph, for example a dermoscopic image or a micrograph, select <strong>Not a clinical photograph</strong> in an image quality task, or give that reason under <strong>Unable to assess these photos</strong> in a visible findings task.</li>
<li>If you see a face, a tattoo, a name, legible text or any other identifying detail, select <strong>Report Privacy Concern</strong>. The photo is paused immediately.</li>
<li>A description of visible findings is not a diagnosis.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="task-types">Task Types<a class="heading-anchor" href="#task-types" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Task</th>
<th>Unit</th>
<th>What you record</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Image quality</td>
<td>One photograph</td>
<td>Whether the photo can be assessed, and why not</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Visible findings</td>
<td>All photos of one case</td>
<td>Regions around visible findings, with descriptive terms</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Diagnosis verification</td>
<td>One case</td>
<td>In preparation (Planned). It will require verified clinical credentials</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<h2 id="image-quality">Image Quality<a class="heading-anchor" href="#image-quality" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Choose one rating:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Rating</th>
<th>Use it when</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Assessable</td>
<td>The finding of interest is visible clearly enough to describe.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Partially assessable</td>
<td>Some of the finding can be described, but part is obscured, out of focus or out of frame.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Unable to assess</td>
<td>The photograph cannot be assessed.</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Then select any reasons that apply: <strong>Obstruction</strong>, <strong>Poor focus</strong>, <strong>Inadequate lighting</strong>, <strong>Not a clinical photograph</strong>. You can add a short note.</p>
<p><strong>Unable to assess</strong> needs a reason: select a flag or write a note. It records the photo&#39;s quality only. It never means that no lesion is present.</p>
<h2 id="visible-findings">Visible Findings<a class="heading-anchor" href="#visible-findings" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Mark each visible finding as a separate region.</p>
<ol>
<li>Choose <strong>Box</strong> or <strong>Polygon</strong> and outline the finding on the photo where you see it. Each region belongs to exactly one photo.</li>
<li>Choose one or more terms that describe the finding.</li>
<li>Set <strong>Certain</strong> or <strong>Uncertain</strong>.</li>
<li>Optionally add a note. Notes are stored with your annotation and are not published.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="box-or-polygon">Box or Polygon<a class="heading-anchor" href="#box-or-polygon" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<ul>
<li>Use a <strong>box</strong> for a compact finding. It is quickest to draw and to check.</li>
<li>Use a <strong>polygon</strong> when the outline is irregular, or when a box would take in a lot of unaffected skin.</li>
<li>Outline the whole finding and no more.</li>
</ul>
<p>The platform checks every shape before you can submit:</p>
<ul>
<li>A box must have positive width and height.</li>
<li>A polygon needs at least three distinct vertices, no repeated consecutive vertices, a positive area and no crossing edges.</li>
<li>Every point must lie inside the photo.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="terms">Terms<a class="heading-anchor" href="#terms" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>Choose terms from the morphology vocabulary, up to 12 per region. Filter the list by name; each term shows a short definition.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Group</th>
<th>Terms</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Primary lesions</td>
<td>Macule, Patch, Papule, Plaque, Nodule, Vesicle, Bulla, Pustule, Wheal, Cyst</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Secondary changes</td>
<td>Scale, Crust, Erosion, Ulcer, Fissure, Excoriation, Lichenification, Atrophy, Scar, Eschar or necrosis</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Vascular and purpuric</td>
<td>Petechiae, Purpura, Telangiectasia</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Arrangement and shape</td>
<td>Annular, Grouped or clustered, Linear, Umbilicated</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p><strong>This vocabulary is a DRAFT</strong> (version 0.1.0-draft). It has not been clinically approved. The clinical lead and vocabulary maintainers must approve its names, definitions and size conventions before a real annotation batch opens. The workspace labels it &quot;Vocabulary 0.1.0-draft (draft)&quot;. Size cut-offs, such as macule versus patch, vary between references and often cannot be measured in a photograph. The approved guide will say how to handle unknown size.</p>
<p><strong>Term Not Listed.</strong> If no term fits, describe the finding in a few words under <strong>Term Not Listed</strong>. Every region needs at least one term or a &quot;Term not listed&quot; description. Reviewers see these descriptions. They are excluded from public release packages.</p>
<h3 id="certain-or-uncertain">Certain or Uncertain<a class="heading-anchor" href="#certain-or-uncertain" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>Every region starts as <strong>Certain</strong>. Choose <strong>Uncertain</strong> when you are not sure that the region shows the finding, or that the chosen terms fit. The reviewer sees your choice, and it is kept in releases.</p>
<h3 id="finding-without-location">Finding Without Location<a class="heading-anchor" href="#finding-without-location" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>Select <strong>Finding Without Location</strong> when you can describe a finding but cannot mark where it is on the photo. The region is recorded as <strong>Cannot localize</strong>. It still needs a term or a &quot;Term not listed&quot; description.</p>
<h3 id="unable-to-assess-these-photos">Unable to Assess These Photos<a class="heading-anchor" href="#unable-to-assess-these-photos" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>If none of the photos can be assessed, tick <strong>Unable to assess these photos</strong> and explain why. Remove any findings first; the two cannot be combined. Like the image quality rating, it records only that the photos could not be assessed. It never means that no lesion is present.</p>
<h2 id="independent-assessment">Independent Assessment<a class="heading-anchor" href="#independent-assessment" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>During an independent assessment you see only the photographs, the instructions and your own annotations. Model predictions, other people&#39;s annotations and any answer supplied with the task are hidden.</p>
<p>Some queues collect several independent assessments of each case; the queue card shows how many. Each assessment is reviewed on its own.</p>
<p>An assisted mode, which would show model suggestions and record the model, its version and your edits, is Planned.</p>
<h2 id="after-you-submit">After You Submit<a class="heading-anchor" href="#after-you-submit" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>An independent clinical reviewer checks your work. You see the outcome in your workspace. See <a href="/docs/annotation/review">How review works</a>.</p>
