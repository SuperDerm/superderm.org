1f:T15eb,<h1>How the Preprint&#39;s Evaluation Differs</h1>
<p>The SuperDerm preprint reports results under its own scoring protocol. Challenges on this platform use the <a href="/docs/evaluation/scoring">community scoring protocol</a>. Both score free-text diagnoses, but they match answers to the documented diagnosis differently. A challenge score is therefore not directly comparable with a number in the preprint.</p>
<h2 id="what-is-the-same">What Is the Same<a class="heading-anchor" href="#what-is-the-same" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>Both score free-text differentials: a ranked list of diagnoses for each case.</li>
<li>Both use the same metric definitions: top-k accuracy over all cases, and macro top-k recall averaged over diagnoses. A missing answer scores zero.</li>
<li>Both use the preprint&#39;s taxonomy of 214 diagnoses and its rare and high-consequence designations, made by majority vote of five physicians.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-answers-are-matched">How Answers Are Matched<a class="heading-anchor" href="#how-answers-are-matched" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th></th>
<th>Preprint protocol</th>
<th>Community protocol</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Names and synonyms</td>
<td>An exact name, or a synonym from the preprint&#39;s dictionary, which maps 21,026 source names to the 214 diagnoses, scores 1</td>
<td>Canonical names plus 63 platform synonyms, marked draft until clinically approved</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>NOS reference</td>
<td>When a case is labelled NOS (&quot;not otherwise specified&quot;), a more specific diagnosis in the same subgroup is also accepted</td>
<td>No subtype credit: naming a more specific diagnosis than an NOS reference scores 0</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>An answer naming another diagnosis</td>
<td>Scores 0</td>
<td>Scores 0</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Unresolved answers</td>
<td>A large language model acting as judge (Qwen3.8-27B, given the subgroup and category) classifies the answer as equivalent, subtype, parent, sibling or different. Equivalents and subtypes are accepted</td>
<td>No judge. Matching is deterministic after normalization, and unmapped or ambiguous answers score 0</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Multiple choice</td>
<td>Each case is also asked as a paired four-option question (MCQ) with the same inputs</td>
<td>Open-ended only. Challenges have no multiple-choice question</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>The preprint&#39;s protocol is described in its Section 3.2, &quot;Scoring Free-Text Answers&quot;.</p>
<h2 id="further-community-rules">Further Community Rules<a class="heading-anchor" href="#further-community-rules" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Strict files.</strong> Two names for the same diagnosis in one list, more than three answers, or unknown, repeated or missing case IDs reject the whole file instead of being scored.</li>
<li><strong>Explicit abstention.</strong> An empty list is allowed. It counts as a miss and stays in the denominator.</li>
<li><strong>Cohorts are challenge labels</strong>, assigned to each case in the reference file, for example <code>common_mimic</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Macro recall covers only diagnoses present</strong> in the challenge&#39;s reference set. Results report <code>n_diagnoses</code> and <code>n_diagnoses_in_set</code> so the difference is visible.</li>
<li><strong>Point estimates only.</strong> There are no confidence intervals, and leaderboard ties are exact ties of the published values.</li>
<li><strong>Reproducible offline.</strong> Anyone can recompute a score from the published scorer, the vocabulary and the same files. Every result records the scorer and vocabulary versions and content hashes.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-they-differ">Why They Differ<a class="heading-anchor" href="#why-they-differ" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>A challenge score must be reproducible by anyone, offline and byte for byte, without a language model or the preprint&#39;s full dictionary. The community protocol therefore uses fixed, published matching rules. Because its synonym list is a versioned draft, every result names the vocabulary version it used.</p>
<h2 id="reporting-results">Reporting Results<a class="heading-anchor" href="#reporting-results" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>Label preprint results as author-reported, and name the model, the data split and the protocol behind each number. The official model pages do this.</li>
<li>Report a community score with its scorer version, challenge version and vocabulary version. When online evaluation opens (Planned), each published score will link to its evaluation run.</li>
<li>Never rank preprint numbers and community scores together.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="training-exposure">Training Exposure<a class="heading-anchor" href="#training-exposure" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>The preprint&#39;s photographs without diagnostic labels were used to train the SuperDerm visual encoder. Adding labels to them later does not make them suitable for a new blinded test. New blind evaluations favour cases with documented provenance and no prior use in training, retrieval indexes, development sets, demonstrations or annotation tasks.</p>
<p>The full scorer rules are in <a href="https://github.com/SuperDerm/SuperDerm-Release/blob/main/evaluator/README.md"><code>evaluator/README.md</code></a> in the SuperDerm-Release repository. See also the <a href="/models/superderm-scorer">SuperDerm community scorer</a> page and the <a href="/docs/evaluation/scoring">community scoring protocol</a>.</p>
