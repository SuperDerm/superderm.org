SuperDerm Documentation

SuperDerm is an open research community for clinical photographs of skin disease. Clinicians and researchers use it to contribute documented cases, annotate visible findings, and build and evaluate models for common and rare skin diseases.

SuperDerm works only with clinical photographs taken with a phone or camera. Dermoscopic images, histopathology micrographs and images of uncertain modality go to a data steward during intake and never enter the community annotation workflow.

For research discussion only. Everything on SuperDerm, including model outputs, is for research discussion only. It is not medical advice and does not constitute a medical diagnosis. If you have a concern about your skin, see a qualified clinician.

Where Your Data Goes#

Photographs imported into SuperDerm follow this path:

  1. Files go to private staging storage. Storage keys are random, and original file names stay in restricted records.
  2. A background worker checks each file, applies the camera's orientation, removes embedded metadata and makes a derived copy.
  3. A data steward reviews every derived image for faces, tattoos, legible text and other identifying details.
  4. Each use, such as annotation or redistribution, must be permitted by the source's registered rights. Unknown uses stay closed.
  5. Cleared photos can enter annotation queues. They are shown only through short-lived links that are checked on every request.
  6. An independent clinical reviewer checks every annotation. Approved work enters a versioned release only if its photographs are cleared for redistribution.

Photos attached to a contributed case stay in private staging until the case is reviewed. Review of contributions is Planned; see Contribute a case.

What "Planned" Means#

These pages describe the platform as it works today. A feature that is designed but not yet available is marked Planned. Do not rely on a Planned feature until this documentation describes it as available.

Preview Environments#

Preview environments show a banner at the top of every page: "Preview environment. Synthetic photographs, fixture answers and unconfirmed credits are labelled. Not for public release." Photographs shown on the site are credited in Image credits.
