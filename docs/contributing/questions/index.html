Propose a Clinical Question

Use Propose a clinical question to tell the community which skin conditions are hard to tell apart in your setting. You do not need any cases to propose a question. Write in everyday clinical language; no technical knowledge is needed.

The form page gives this example:

Early mpox can look like varicella or molluscum. Missing it delays isolation and testing. A model should list mpox in its top three from one or two photos and a short history.

Fields#

The Clinical Question#

Field Required Example or notes
Title Yes "Early mpox versus varicella in children"
Presentations that are difficult to tell apart Yes Which conditions are confused, and how they present
Why it matters: consequences of missing it, and priority populations Yes Harm from a missed or late diagnosis, and who is most affected

What a Model Would Do#

All optional.

Field Example
Inputs One or more photos and a short history
Desired outputs Top three diagnoses
Cases or annotations needed What evidence a test set would require
Resources already available Collections, publications or tools you know of
Work you can take on Contribute cases, review annotations, act as clinical lead

Submit#

  1. Sign in and accept the community rules; see Getting started.
  2. Fill in the form and select Preview. Missing fields are listed at the top, each linked to its field.
  3. Select Submit for Review.

Your receipt shows a submission ID that stays the same through revisions, and the proposal appears under Contributions in My workspace. To continue a saved draft later, select Resume Draft there.

From Proposal to Challenge#

The form page shows the path a question follows:

  1. Proposed. You submit the question.
  2. Accepted by the clinical team. A proposal can also need more information or be deferred. The original proposer and the reason for each decision are kept.
  3. In Preparation. A clinical lead defines which cases are eligible, what evidence the reference diagnosis needs, and the common conditions it is confused with. An evaluation maintainer prepares development examples, data splits, the scorer and a reproducible baseline.
  4. Open for Contributions. The challenge collects cases and annotations.
  5. Open for Evaluation. Prediction submission opens only when the input package, terms of use, reference answers and scorer version are ready and the baseline has been rerun successfully.

Afterwards a challenge is Closed, then Archived.

Review tools for proposals are Planned. Until they are available, your proposal stays in Pending Review.

The First Challenge#

Rare Skin Disease and Common Mimics is In Preparation. Its question: given clinical photographs taken with a phone or camera, can a model include a rare, high-consequence condition in its top three when it resembles a common presentation?

  • The clinical team will select the first set of diagnoses and the common conditions they resemble.
  • Its owner and evaluation maintainer are still to be confirmed.
  • You can help now by contributing documented cases and annotations.
  • Submit Predictions stays disabled until evaluation opens. The scoring rules are already published: see Community scoring protocol.

Credit#

Challenge credit is Planned. Each challenge will credit its clinical lead, evaluation maintainer, proposer, case contributors and annotators according to each person's attribution preference. See Contribution and credit.
