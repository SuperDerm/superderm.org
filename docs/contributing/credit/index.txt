1f:T11a2,<h1>Contribution and Credit</h1>
<p>SuperDerm credits the people who do the work, and you decide how your name appears.</p>
<h2 id="choose-how-you-are-credited">Choose How You Are Credited<a class="heading-anchor" href="#choose-how-you-are-credited" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>You first choose when you accept the community rules. Change it at any time under <strong>Profile and Credit</strong> in <a href="/contribute">My workspace</a>, then select <strong>Save Profile</strong>.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Choice</th>
<th>Public credit shows</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Display name (the default)</td>
<td>Your display name</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Full name</td>
<td>The <strong>Full name</strong> in your profile, which is shown only with this choice</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Anonymous contributor</td>
<td>&quot;Anonymous contributor&quot;</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>If you choose full name but leave <strong>Full name</strong> empty, you are credited as &quot;Anonymous contributor&quot;. The top of your workspace always shows your current public credit.</p>
<p>Your email address and contact details are never shown publicly. You can also add an ORCID iD, an affiliation and a short biography. These are self-reported, grant no permissions, and are not shown on public pages today.</p>
<h2 id="public-contributor-list">Public Contributor List<a class="heading-anchor" href="#public-contributor-list" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>The homepage lists community contributors in its <strong>About SuperDerm</strong> section. You appear there only if all three are true:</p>
<ul>
<li>you ticked <strong>List me in the public contributor list</strong> in your profile (it is off by default);</li>
<li>your credit choice is not anonymous;</li>
<li>at least one of your annotations has been approved by a reviewer.</li>
</ul>
<p>The list shows your public credit and nothing else. The platform does not rank contributors.</p>
<h2 id="credit-in-releases">Credit in Releases<a class="heading-anchor" href="#credit-in-releases" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>When reviewed work ships in a published release:</p>
<ol>
<li>You get a notification, &quot;Your work is in release …&quot;, with the number of your reviewed annotations it includes.</li>
<li><strong>Releases That Include Your Work</strong> in your workspace lists each version, your number of annotations and your credit.</li>
<li>Inside the package:<ul>
<li>every row of <code>annotations.jsonl</code> has a <code>credit</code> field with the annotator&#39;s public credit;</li>
<li><code>DATASET_CARD.md</code> lists each credited name with its number of reviewed results;</li>
<li><code>CITATION.cff</code> lists the authors as &quot;SuperDerm contributors&quot;.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Credit is taken from your preference when the package is built. Published packages never change, so a later change of preference applies to future releases only.</p>
<p>Package credit names the annotator whose work passed review. A reviewer&#39;s edits do not change who is credited. Reviewers are not listed in package credit.</p>
<p>See <a href="/docs/reference/release-format">Release package format</a> for every field.</p>
<h2 id="cases-datasets-models-and-questions">Cases, Datasets, Models and Questions<a class="heading-anchor" href="#cases-datasets-models-and-questions" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Credit for these contributions will follow the same preference. It appears once contribution review is available, which is Planned. Dataset and model entries will also show the authors, institution and required attribution you enter in their forms.</p>
<h2 id="licence-of-your-contributions">Licence of Your Contributions<a class="heading-anchor" href="#licence-of-your-contributions" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Under the contribution terms you accept, annotations you submit may be reviewed, corrected and included in versioned releases under the release licence shown on each release. The current terms are labelled as a draft under review; the version you accepted is recorded with your account.</p>
<h2 id="withdrawal">Withdrawal<a class="heading-anchor" href="#withdrawal" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>You can ask to withdraw your contributions. The request workflow is Planned. Copies already downloaded outside the platform cannot be recalled. See <a href="/docs/data/access-and-withdrawal">Data access and withdrawal</a>.</p>
