Contribution and Credit

SuperDerm credits the people who do the work, and you decide how your name appears.

Choose How You Are Credited#

You first choose when you accept the community rules. Change it at any time under Profile and Credit in My workspace, then select Save Profile.

Choice Public credit shows
Display name (the default) Your display name
Full name The Full name in your profile, which is shown only with this choice
Anonymous contributor "Anonymous contributor"

If you choose full name but leave Full name empty, you are credited as "Anonymous contributor". The top of your workspace always shows your current public credit.

Your email address and contact details are never shown publicly. You can also add an ORCID iD, an affiliation and a short biography. These are self-reported, grant no permissions, and are not shown on public pages today.

Public Contributor List#

The homepage lists community contributors in its About SuperDerm section. You appear there only if all three are true:

  • you ticked List me in the public contributor list in your profile (it is off by default);
  • your credit choice is not anonymous;
  • at least one of your annotations has been approved by a reviewer.

The list shows your public credit and nothing else. The platform does not rank contributors.

Credit in Releases#

When reviewed work ships in a published release:

  1. You get a notification, "Your work is in release …", with the number of your reviewed annotations it includes.
  2. Releases That Include Your Work in your workspace lists each version, your number of annotations and your credit.
  3. Inside the package:
    • every row of annotations.jsonl has a credit field with the annotator's public credit;
    • DATASET_CARD.md lists each credited name with its number of reviewed results;
    • CITATION.cff lists the authors as "SuperDerm contributors".

Credit is taken from your preference when the package is built. Published packages never change, so a later change of preference applies to future releases only.

Package credit names the annotator whose work passed review. A reviewer's edits do not change who is credited. Reviewers are not listed in package credit.

See Release package format for every field.

Cases, Datasets, Models and Questions#

Credit for these contributions will follow the same preference. It appears once contribution review is available, which is Planned. Dataset and model entries will also show the authors, institution and required attribution you enter in their forms.

Licence of Your Contributions#

Under the contribution terms you accept, annotations you submit may be reviewed, corrected and included in versioned releases under the release licence shown on each release. The current terms are labelled as a draft under review; the version you accepted is recorded with your account.

Withdrawal#

You can ask to withdraw your contributions. The request workflow is Planned. Copies already downloaded outside the platform cannot be recalled. See Data access and withdrawal.
