Contribute a Case

Use Contribute a case to share one case with one or more clinical photographs. Rare conditions are especially valuable, and so are the common conditions that resemble them. The disease can be unknown: authorized photographs can still be annotated for visible findings.

This form is for research contributions. It does not give medical advice.

Who Can Contribute#

Clinical teams, dataset authors, and patients or their authorized representatives. Everyone uses the same restricted intake. You must be signed in and have accepted the community rules; see Getting started.

Before You Start#

  • Photos must be clinical photographs from a phone or camera, in JPEG or PNG, up to 25 MiB and 80 million pixels each. Export HEIC/HEIF photos as JPEG first. Dermoscopic and histopathology images do not belong here.
  • Leave names, dates of birth, record numbers and other identifiers out of every text field.
  • Know which uses your consent or agreement covers.

About This Contribution#

Field Required Notes
Short title for your records Yes Not published.
Contribution category Yes Rare skin condition (rare), Common condition that resembles a rare one (common_mimic), Other skin condition (other), Not sure (unsure)
Your role Yes Clinical team, Dataset author, Patient sharing my own case, Authorized representative, Other
Contact email Yes Prefilled from your account. Kept in restricted records.

Contribute a Case only preselects the rare category. The clinical lead verifies rare-disease status.

Case Description#

All optional: a brief history (onset, duration, symptoms, relevant context), an age band, the body site, and conditions it resembles. The last helps match rare conditions with their common mimics.

Diagnostic Evidence#

Field Notes
Diagnosis as documented by the source Optional. Leave it empty if unknown. Unfamiliar rare-disease names are kept as entered.
How was it established? Required when you give a diagnosis: Unknown, Pathology, Laboratory testing, Genetic testing, Clinical records, Published case, Other source record.
When was it obtained? Optional. Approximate is fine, such as "2025" or "unknown".

Each item may be unknown; choose Unknown rather than guessing. A diagnosis without supporting evidence is recorded as reported, pending verification by a clinical reviewer. Do not attach supporting documents: the restricted evidence workflow for them is Planned.

Sharing Permissions#

Tick only the uses your consent or agreement covers. Unknown uses stay closed. The first two start ticked.

Option Use
Viewing on the platform by authorized contributors view
Community annotation on the platform annotate
Training research models train
Blinded evaluation (challenges) evaluate
Public display as a teaching case display
Redistribution in downloadable releases redistribute

Also required: the basis for these permissions (for example, written patient consent for research use and publication, with the form version) and the source of the photographs (for example, your clinic, or a published case report with its citation). See Data access and withdrawal for how uses are enforced.

Photographs#

Add one or more photos. Each upload goes to private staging, and the platform checks that the full file arrived. The thumbnails you see exist only in your browser. A data steward reviews photos for identifying details before any use.

Save, Preview and Submit#

  1. Select Save Draft at any time. The first save creates your submission ID: "Draft saved · SUB-…".
  2. Select Preview. Missing or invalid fields are listed at the top, each linked to its field.
  3. Check the preview, then select Submit for Review, or Back to Edit.

The browser warns you before you leave with unsaved changes. To continue a saved draft later, open Contributions in My workspace and select Resume Draft. The form reopens with your saved answers and shows "Draft SUB-… reopened", and the same submission ID continues through to the receipt. Only you can reopen your draft, and a submitted revision cannot be reopened.

Your Receipt#

After submitting you see Received for Review with the submission ID (SUB- and ten characters), the status Pending Review and the revision number. Keep the ID: it stays the same if a reviewer asks for more information and you send a revision. You also get a notification, and the case appears under Contributions in My workspace. A submitted revision is locked.

What Happens Next#

A contribution can be Draft, Pending review, In review, More information required, Accepted, Partially accepted, Declined or Withdrawn.

Reviewer tools for contributions are Planned. Until they are available, a submitted case stays in Pending Review, and its photos stay in private staging. When review opens:

  • a data steward checks the permissions and any identifying details in the text and photos;
  • the clinical lead verifies rare-disease status and the reference diagnosis against the evidence;
  • a request for more information opens a new revision under the same ID, and the history is kept;
  • an accepted case follows the photo path in Data and privacy. If annotation is permitted, it can enter annotation queues, even with an unknown diagnosis.

Credit for your contribution follows your attribution choice; see Contribution and credit.
