22:T1971,<h1>Data Access and Withdrawal</h1>
<p>Every photograph carries a record of what it may be used for. The platform applies the same record in the interface and on the server, on every request.</p>
<h2 id="use-categories">Use Categories<a class="heading-anchor" href="#use-categories" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Use</th>
<th>Permits</th>
<th>Where the current build enforces it</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td><code>catalog</code></td>
<td>A catalog entry or external link only; no image is sent</td>
<td>Recorded. The only use allowed before privacy review</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>view</code></td>
<td>Viewing on the platform by authorized contributors</td>
<td>Recorded. A separate view-only mode is Planned</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>annotate</code></td>
<td>Community annotation on the platform</td>
<td>Queue counts, claims, the media gateway for annotators and reviewers, and submission</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>display</code></td>
<td>Public display as a teaching case</td>
<td>Recorded. Teaching cases are a separately approved list</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>redistribute</code></td>
<td>Inclusion in downloadable releases</td>
<td>Release freeze and package build</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>train</code></td>
<td>Training research models</td>
<td>Recorded. Training manifests are Planned</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>evaluate</code></td>
<td>Blinded evaluation in challenges</td>
<td>Recorded. Challenge evaluation is Planned</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Each use is decided separately. Permission to annotate says nothing about redistribution or training.</p>
<h2 id="closed-by-default">Closed by Default<a class="heading-anchor" href="#closed-by-default" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>A use is allowed only when all of these hold:</p>
<ol>
<li>The source, the case and the photo are active: not paused, suspended or withdrawn.</li>
<li>For every use except <code>catalog</code>, the photo&#39;s derived image has passed privacy review.</li>
<li>The source explicitly allows the use, and that decision has not expired or been revoked.</li>
<li>The photo&#39;s rights statement (the <code>rights_ref</code> in a manifest) allows the use, and has not expired.</li>
<li>No narrower decision on the case or the photo blocks it.</li>
</ol>
<p>An unknown or missing decision keeps the use closed.</p>
<h2 id="narrowing-only">Narrowing Only<a class="heading-anchor" href="#narrowing-only" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>A case or a single photo can carry its own decision for a use. It can only narrow what the source and rights statement allow.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Decision on a case or photo</th>
<th>Effect</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>None, or <code>inherit</code></td>
<td>Follows the source and rights statement</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>deny</code> or <code>unknown</code></td>
<td>Blocks the use</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Expired or revoked</td>
<td>Blocks the use</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>allow</code></td>
<td>Cannot loosen a restriction above it</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Annotation queues also skip photos reserved for development, test, blind-test or teaching sets, whatever their permissions.</p>
<h2 id="getting-access">Getting Access<a class="heading-anchor" href="#getting-access" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Public releases.</strong> Anyone can download a published public release package.</li>
<li><strong>Annotation.</strong> Sign in, accept the rules, join the project, pass the practice task and claim a task. You can see a photo only through an assignment that includes it. See <a href="/docs/getting-started/overview">Getting started</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Review.</strong> Hold the clinical reviewer role for the project. You see only photos of tasks you did not work on, from cases you did not contribute.</li>
<li><strong>Controlled access.</strong> Requests for controlled material, naming the use, version, purpose and period, are Planned.</li>
</ul>
<p>When a reviewer or staff role is revoked or expires, the photos you could see through it stop loading. See <a href="/docs/getting-started/roles">Roles and permissions</a>.</p>
<h2 id="withdrawal">Withdrawal<a class="heading-anchor" href="#withdrawal" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<h3 id="effects">Effects<a class="heading-anchor" href="#effects" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>When a source, case or photo is marked withdrawn:</p>
<ul>
<li>the media gateway stops serving its photos on the next request, and any ticket already issued expires within 5 minutes;</li>
<li>its tasks stop being claimable, open tasks that include it cannot be submitted, and the workspace shows that the photo is no longer available;</li>
<li>public counts drop it at once, because they are computed from current records;</li>
<li>it is excluded from any release frozen afterwards. If a photo loses redistribution permission between the freeze and the build, the build fails and the release manager starts a new draft.</li>
</ul>
<p>Published packages are immutable and are not edited. Withdrawing a published release, with a notice and a replacement manifest, is Planned. The download link already handles it: a withdrawn release returns its withdrawal notice instead of the file.</p>
<h3 id="asking-for-withdrawal">Asking for Withdrawal<a class="heading-anchor" href="#asking-for-withdrawal" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>A withdrawal or narrowing request from the item&#39;s record, returning a ticket ID, is Planned. Until it is available:</p>
<ul>
<li>to stop a photo being shown at once, report it with the privacy category; it is paused immediately;</li>
<li>for anything else, send the request through <a href="/docs/data/issues">Report an issue</a>, with the ID of the item.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="what-cannot-be-recalled">What Cannot Be Recalled<a class="heading-anchor" href="#what-cannot-be-recalled" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h3>
<p>Copies already downloaded outside the platform cannot be recalled. This is why withdrawal notices will state their scope. The planned workflow keeps a minimal audit record of each withdrawal, while patient details and authorization documents stay private.</p>
<h2 id="intended-use">Intended Use<a class="heading-anchor" href="#intended-use" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Releases and access are for research only. SuperDerm is not intended for clinical, diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.</p>
