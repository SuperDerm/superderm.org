Roles and Permissions

Every request is checked against your role, your access to the project or collection, and the permitted uses of the material. Roles are granted per project. Only the site administrator role is site-wide.

Roles#

Role What it lets you do How you get it
Visitor Read public pages, guides, catalog entries, challenge descriptions and teaching cases. Download published release packages. No account needed.
Contributor Submit cases, datasets, models and questions. Join the annotation project, take practice tasks, and claim and submit eligible annotation work. Report issues. Sign in with an email link and accept the community rules. See Getting started.
Clinical contributor Claim queues marked Requires verified clinical contributor. Diagnosis verification will require this role (Planned). Granted after credential verification.
Clinical reviewer Review submitted annotations in the review queue. See How review works. Appointed by the clinical lead of the project.
Clinical lead Appoint clinical reviewers. Configure, open, pause and close annotation batches. Granted by a site administrator.
Data steward Import collections through controlled intake, review derived images for privacy, configure and open batches, and act on privacy reports. Granted by a site administrator.
Release manager Create, freeze and publish versioned releases. Granted by a site administrator.
Challenge editor Maintain challenge definitions and scorer versions. Tools for this role are Planned. Granted by a site administrator.
Terminology maintainer Map unfamiliar names to vocabulary terms and maintain vocabulary versions. Tools for this role are Planned. Granted by a site administrator.
Site administrator Grant and revoke any role, and read the audit log. Granted by a site administrator.

Your active roles appear as badges at the top of My workspace. Reviewers also see Review Queue, and staff see Administration, in the My Workspace menu.

Staff Pages#

Each administration page checks its own role, and every action still passes the access checks of the resource it touches.

Page Who can open it
Imports, Privacy review Data steward
Batches Data steward or clinical lead
Releases Release manager
People and roles Site administrator or clinical lead
Audit log Site administrator, data steward or release manager
Overview, Issues Any of the staff roles above

Clinical Credential Verification#

  • Clinical credentials are verified separately from sign-in. A verified email address only shows that you control the address.
  • A self-reported title grants nothing. Your display name, full name, affiliation and biography never unlock a queue, the review queue or a staff page.
  • Whoever grants a role must record how eligibility was verified, for example a licence checked against a registry, or a project invitation. They may also set an expiry date. You receive a notification when a role is granted.
  • Clinical reviewers are appointed by the clinical lead for their project. Other roles are granted by a site administrator, who can also record a reviewer appointment.

How to ask for verification. An in-app verification request is Planned. Until it is available, roles are granted by invitation from the project team.

Independence Rules#

  • You never give a second assessment of a task you have already worked on.
  • You are never offered a case you contributed, for annotation or for review.
  • Reviewers never see tasks they annotated or cases they contributed.
  • Reviewers see annotators as "Assessor 1", "Assessor 2" and so on, not by name.

Expiry and Revocation#

  • A role with an expiry date stops applying when the date passes.
  • A site administrator can revoke any role, with a recorded reason. The change applies from your next request.
  • When a role is revoked, your open claims in queues that required that role are released back to the queue, and their photos stop loading for you.
  • When a reviewer or staff role ends, photos you could see through that role stop loading, because the media gateway rechecks permission on every request. See Data and privacy.
  • Every grant and revocation is written to the audit log with the actor, the reason and the time.
