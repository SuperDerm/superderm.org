22:T1731,<h1>Roles and Permissions</h1>
<p>Every request is checked against your role, your access to the project or collection, and the permitted uses of the material. Roles are granted per project. Only the site administrator role is site-wide.</p>
<h2 id="roles">Roles<a class="heading-anchor" href="#roles" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>

<td>No account needed.</td>

<h2 id="clinical-credential-verification">Clinical Credential Verification<a class="heading-anchor" href="#clinical-credential-verification" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>Clinical credentials are verified separately from sign-in. A verified email address only shows that you control the address.</li>
<li>A self-reported title grants nothing. Your display name, full name, affiliation and biography never unlock a queue, the review queue or a staff page.</li>
<li>Whoever grants a role must record how eligibility was verified, for example a licence checked against a registry, or a project invitation. They may also set an expiry date. You receive a notification when a role is granted.</li>
<li>Clinical reviewers are appointed by the clinical lead for their project. Other roles are granted by a site administrator, who can also record a reviewer appointment.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>How to ask for verification.</strong> An in-app verification request is Planned. Until it is available, roles are granted by invitation from the project team.</p>
<h2 id="independence-rules">Independence Rules<a class="heading-anchor" href="#independence-rules" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>You never give a second assessment of a task you have already worked on.</li>
<li>You are never offered a case you contributed, for annotation or for review.</li>
<li>Reviewers never see tasks they annotated or cases they contributed.</li>
<li>Reviewers see annotators as &quot;Assessor 1&quot;, &quot;Assessor 2&quot; and so on, not by name.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="expiry-and-revocation">Expiry and Revocation<a class="heading-anchor" href="#expiry-and-revocation" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>A role with an expiry date stops applying when the date passes.</li>
<li>A site administrator can revoke any role, with a recorded reason. The change applies from your next request.</li>
<li>When a role is revoked, your open claims in queues that required that role are released back to the queue, and their photos stop loading for you.</li>
<li>When a reviewer or staff role ends, photos you could see through that role stop loading, because the media gateway rechecks permission on every request. See <a href="/docs/data/privacy">Data and privacy</a>.</li>
<li>Every grant and revocation is written to the audit log with the actor, the reason and the time.</li>
</ul>
