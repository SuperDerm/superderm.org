1f:T1993,<h1>Getting Started</h1>
<p>To annotate photos you need an account, the accepted community rules, membership of the annotation project and a passed practice task. Steps 1 to 3 happen once. Step 4 happens once per queue and guide version.</p>
<h2 id="1-sign-in-with-an-email-link">1. Sign In with an Email Link<a class="heading-anchor" href="#1-sign-in-with-an-email-link" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ol>
<li>Go to <a href="/contribute">Sign in</a> and enter your email address.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Email Me a Sign-In Link</strong>.</li>
<li>Open the email &quot;Your SuperDerm sign-in link&quot; and follow the link.</li>
<li>On the <strong>Confirm Sign-In</strong> page, select <strong>Continue to SuperDerm</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>What you will see: you return to the page you started from, or to <a href="/contribute">My workspace</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li>There is no password. Signing in also verifies your email address.</li>
<li>A link works once and expires after 20 minutes. If it has expired, request a new one.</li>
<li>You can request up to 5 links for one address in 15 minutes.</li>
<li>A session lasts 30 days on that device. To sign out, open <strong>My Workspace</strong> at the top right and select <strong>Sign Out</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>In a development or preview environment without outgoing email, the link appears in the development mailbox instead of your inbox. See <a href="/docs/data/privacy#development-mailbox">Data and privacy</a>.</p>
<h2 id="2-accept-the-community-rules">2. Accept the Community Rules<a class="heading-anchor" href="#2-accept-the-community-rules" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>The first time you annotate or contribute, you are sent to <strong>Before You Contribute</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Read the community rules and the contribution terms. Each shows its version.</li>
<li>Enter a display name of at least 2 characters.</li>
<li>Choose how you are credited: display name, full name, or &quot;Anonymous contributor&quot;.</li>
<li>Tick the acceptance box and select <strong>Accept and Continue</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The platform records the exact versions you accepted. The current text is labelled as a draft under review. In short, the rules ask you to:</p>
<ul>
<li>share only photographs you have the right to share, for the uses you state;</li>
<li>leave out names, record numbers, faces, tattoos and other identifiers, and report any you see;</li>
<li>describe what is visible, because a diagnosis suggested from photographs alone is a hypothesis;</li>
<li>keep independent assessments independent;</li>
<li>remember that SuperDerm gives no medical advice.</li>
</ul>
<p>You can change your credit choice later. See <a href="/docs/contributing/credit">Contribution and credit</a>.</p>
<h2 id="3-join-the-annotation-project">3. Join the Annotation Project<a class="heading-anchor" href="#3-join-the-annotation-project" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>On <a href="/annotate">Annotate photos</a>, select <strong>Join the Annotation Project</strong>. Membership records the project&#39;s participation terms.</p>
<h2 id="4-pass-the-practice-task">4. Pass the Practice Task<a class="heading-anchor" href="#4-pass-the-practice-task" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p>Each queue card lists what you still need under <strong>Requires</strong>. On the queue you want, select <strong>Take the Practice Task</strong>. Practice photographs are synthetic.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Queue type</th>
<th>What you do</th>
<th>To pass</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Visible findings</td>
<td>Outline the single raised finding with a box or polygon, then choose the terms that describe it</td>
<td>Your outline covers the finding closely (an overlap of at least 0.5, measured as intersection over union) and at least one chosen term fits</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Image quality</td>
<td>Rate two photographs and select reasons where needed</td>
<td>Every photograph is rated correctly, with a reason where one is required</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>If you do not pass, the page says what to change. Try again as often as you like. A pass is kept for the project, task type and guide version, and appears under <strong>Qualifications</strong> in your workspace. When a queue moves to a new guide version, take its practice task again.</p>
<p>Diagnosis verification has no practice task. That queue type is in preparation (Planned).</p>
<h2 id="5-claim-a-photo">5. Claim a Photo<a class="heading-anchor" href="#5-claim-a-photo" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<ol>
<li>On a queue card, select <strong>Start Annotating</strong>.</li>
<li>The platform claims one eligible task for you in a single step and opens the annotation workspace.</li>
</ol>
<p>What you will see:</p>
<ul>
<li>The claim is yours for 30 minutes and renews while your work is saved. See <a href="/docs/annotation/workspace">Using the workspace</a>.</li>
<li>If you already hold an open claim in that queue, you get it back instead of a second one. The button then reads <strong>Resume or Claim</strong>, and the card lists your drafts.</li>
<li>You are never offered a task you have already worked on, or a case you contributed.</li>
<li>If nothing is left to claim, you see &quot;Nothing left to claim in this queue right now.&quot; Try another task type or check back later.</li>
<li>A queue marked <strong>Requires verified clinical contributor</strong> needs that role. See <a href="/docs/getting-started/roles">Roles and permissions</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>If no queue is open, the page shows &quot;No photos are available for annotation yet&quot;. Photographs enter queues only after their permissions and privacy review are complete.</p>
<h2 id="your-workspace">Your Workspace<a class="heading-anchor" href="#your-workspace" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a></h2>
<p><a href="/contribute">My workspace</a> shows:</p>
<ul>
<li>notifications, such as receipts, review decisions and release inclusion;</li>
<li>your annotations and drafts, with the next action: <strong>Resume</strong>, <strong>Respond to review</strong>, <strong>View Draft</strong> or <strong>View Result</strong>;</li>
<li>releases that include your work, with your credit;</li>
<li>your contributions, with their tracking IDs, status and a <strong>Resume Draft</strong> link for unsubmitted drafts;</li>
<li>your profile and credit settings, and your practice results.</li>
</ul>
<p>Next: read the <a href="/docs/annotation/guide">annotation guide</a> before your first task.</p>
