# PAD-UFES-20 photographs shown on the site

Clinical close-up photographs from the public **PAD-UFES-20** dataset, shown on the homepage, on Annotate photos and
in the teaching cases.

- **Creators:** Pacheco AGC, Lima GR, Salomão AS, et al. PAD-UFES-20: A skin lesion dataset composed of patient data and
  clinical images collected from smartphones. *Data in Brief.* 2020;32:106221. doi:10.1016/j.dib.2020.106221
- **Source:** Mendeley Data, https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zr7vgbcyr2/1 (doi:10.17632/zr7vgbcyr2.1)
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Changes:** converted from PNG to WebP with metadata removed; images wider than 960 px resized to 960 px. Outlines
  and boxes are drawn by the site on a separate layer.
- **Diagnoses and histories** in the teaching cases are the dataset's recorded values (biopsy result where the dataset
  marks the lesion as biopsied).

File names use the PAD-UFES-20 lesion number and do not name the diagnosis, so the teaching cases are not given away.
