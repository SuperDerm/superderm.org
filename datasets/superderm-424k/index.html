SuperDerm-424K

Clinical photographs of common and rare skin diseases, with diagnosis labels mapped to a 214-diagnosis taxonomy. Phone and camera photographs only; no dermoscopic images.

DatasetImages: Source OnlyMetadata: AvailableAnnotations: Coming Soon

Availability

Released openly in stages. Version 0.1 is available now: labels, splits and quality tiers for all 395,432 photographs that come from 21 public datasets, mapped to the 214-diagnosis taxonomy. Images come from each provider under its own terms; a script in the download links them to the labels. Our clinician-curated collection of over 50,000 cases follows after privacy and permission review.

Prior Use in Training

The 151,209 unlabeled photographs were used to train the SuperDerm visual encoder. Adding labels to them later does not make them suitable for a new blinded test.

Limitations

  • Sources differ in how diagnoses were established; each label keeps its original name, source and the evidence for any correction.
  • Healthy skin appears only in the test set.
  • Photo counts cannot establish how many people are represented.
