Public Datasets

The labels of all of them are in version 0.1. Images stay with each provider.

  • Fitzpatrick 17kClinical images with Fitzpatrick skin type labels.
  • SCINCrowdsourced dermatology images with self-reported information and dermatologist labels.
  • Diverse Dermatology Images (DDI)Curated clinical images with pathology-confirmed diagnoses.
  • PASSIONPigmented skin images from sub-Saharan Africa. Held out as part of the SuperDerm OOD test.
  • PAD-UFES-20Smartphone clinical images with patient data.
  • Derm1MVision-language dermatology dataset aligned with a clinical ontology.
  • SkinCAPDermatology images with medical captions.
  • DermaCon-INIndian skin disorders with multiconcept annotations. Held out as part of the SuperDerm OOD test.
  • SkinDisNetClinical images and metadata. Held out as part of the SuperDerm OOD test.
  • SD-260Clinical skin disease images across 260 classes.
  • SD-198Clinical skin disease images across 198 classes, the predecessor of SD-260.
  • Public Mpox Image SetsMSLD v1 and v2.0, MSID, MPXV-CNN, MPox-Vision, MCSI, MCVSLD, Islam6, Ahsan2022 and UKHSA images.
